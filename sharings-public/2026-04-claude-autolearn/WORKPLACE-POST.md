# Past AI speed: a 4-pattern PE playbook — 4 teams already piloting

Speed is table stakes — every team gets it eventually. The question worth asking *now* is what you do after. Last week at PE Anchor Week I argued for 4 patterns that compound past speed: they change *what work your team can take on*, not just how fast. 15-min talk + 90-min hands-on workshop; 4 PEs picked up patterns to run on their own stacks afterwards.

**The 4 patterns** — each independent, pick the one with the lowest setup cost for you:

| # | Pattern | What changes | Concrete signal from my stack |
|---|---------|--------------|-------------------------------|
| 1 | **Gdoc as the interface** | One doc replaces 35 min of morning tab-opening: meetings, follow-ups, oncall context, peer-activity all surface there | 22 cron jobs, 530 runs/week, 92% autonomous pass rate (+10% over 4 weeks) |
| 2 | **Auto-discovered context** | Org + diffs + GChat + Workplace → people profiles + project maps. Ramp into a new collaborator or area in 30 sec, not 3 days | 40 people profiles built; minutes vs days for new-area ramp |
| 3 | **AI as research engine** | Leave a comment on a shared doc → wake up to a researched answer. Hours of work, overnight | 42 deep dives in 2 weeks — 4-8h topics compressed into ~25 min each |
| 4 | **Autonomous workflow** | No human-in-loop, no human-on-loop. AI proposes diffs from doc comments, drafts GChat replies, self-heals from corrections | 173 opportunities surfaced; 3 diffs auto-created from comments (zero prompts) |

**Try this week** — pick one, each is < 2 hrs:

1. Build **one people profile** (`/my-people` on your manager — 30 min). Pre-loaded context changes every conversation.
2. Write a **weaknesses file** (`PREFS.md`, 3 weaknesses + evidence — 20 min). Coaching becomes proactive, not generic.
3. Set up **one overnight cron** (area monitor — scans your contacts' diffs while you sleep — 1-2 hrs).
