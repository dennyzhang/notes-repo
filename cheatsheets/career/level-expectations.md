# IC Level Expectations Reference

## General IC Expectations

### IC3
- Complete individual tasks independently with minimal guidance
- Show eagerness for more responsibility
- Understand assigned codebase/product area
- **Progression:** Expected to reach IC4 within 24 months

### IC4
- Complete medium to large features independently
- Sustained self-driven productivity
- Strong understanding of assigned codebase; drive changes
- Effective cross-functional collaboration
- **Progression:** Expected to reach IC5 within 33 months

### IC5
- Established steward of components/features/systems
- Expert end-to-end understanding
- Consistently high productivity across many large-scope projects
- Proactive problem-finder; drive resolution without guidance
- Make people around you more efficient
- Lead people toward common goals
- Drive tasks across teams; build cross-team relationships
- Contribute to larger org (recruiting, bootcamp, interns)

### IC6
- Exemplify values; support others in doing right for company
- Work with speed and simplicity; identify roadblocks early
- Solve technical problems few others can; sought out for this ability
- Led at least one major initiative successfully
- Critical role in setting team direction and goals
- Strong oral and written communication
- Influence engineering culture in broader team

## Production Engineer (PE) Specific

### PE Core Principles — Five Pillars
1. **Reliability**: Share load with SWE partners for stable, highly available production
2. **Efficiency**: Increase observability and optimize resources
3. **Scalability**: Modify software to effortlessly scale as demand changes
4. **Security**: Assure security and privacy of the platform
5. **Culture**: Reliability-obsessed, relationship builders, move fast the right way

### IC5 (E5) — Senior PE / Emerging Tech Lead
- Own projects taking at least a full half (measured in months)
- Independently solve whole business problems — figure out both what to do and how
- Delegate while maintaining high individual productivity. "Focusing solely on facilitation or delegating away tech work is not a path to success."
- Drive SLO definition for owned services, adopt SLICK
- Champion Engineering Excellence: SEV contributions, reliability, efficiency, performance, security
- ~25 hours/half on org contributions (interviewing, mentoring, DPE)
- Diff velocity: at least 1 diff/week, aim for 3+/week

**PE Fixer Archetype (IC5):**
- Gravitate toward investigations, tracking recurring problems
- Systematically improve oncall/dashboards/logs/tools
- "If the packet looks like a laundry list, but each item is impactful, you may have a fixer."

### IC6 (E6) — Established PE Tech Lead
- Projects measured in halves or years, influencing other teams
- Leadership is the key differentiator from high-performing IC5
- Start being involved with business aspects
- Partner with managers for roadmaps with ROI
- Pay attention to opportunity costs
- Run roadmap planning, coordinate team-wide events
- Discover new areas for team to pursue
- Encourage service owners to define and maintain SLOs
- Lead programs to drive down tech debt, SEV follow-ups, recurring oncall load

**PE Fixer Archetype (IC6):**
- Natural circle of influence — other Sr ICs and managers listen
- Take on investigations others cannot, usually across multiple systems
- Surface common patterns and help with approach to fix them
- Don't care about organizational boundaries — follow problems
- Become the "go-to" person for certain classes of problems
- Raise the bar in SEV Reviews
- Teach others how to investigate

### PE Tech Lead Responsibilities

| Responsibility | Expectation |
|---------------|-------------|
| **Code review** | "TL diff review is more important than landing diffs." Set a code-block for review. |
| **Shipping code** | "99% of the time you need to continue writing diffs." Prototype, solve hardest parts. |
| **Project management** | Break projects into GSD tasks, weekly sync, track deadlines |
| **Oncall quality** | Own rotation: non-actioned alerts <5%, MTTD/MTTR tracking |
| **SLO management** | Drive SLI/SLO definition, SLICK adoption, SEV-SLI linkage |
| **Capacity efficiency** | Right-sizing, decommission, SKU governance |
| **Upward communication** | Regular updates with metrics, mapped to OKRs |
| **People development** | "Spread scope. Don't hoard fun work." Backstop, don't catch. |
