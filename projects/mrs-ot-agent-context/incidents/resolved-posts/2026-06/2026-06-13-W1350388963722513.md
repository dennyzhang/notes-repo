## Root cause
MRB tool killed and reset mvai-training-online-2137792444 in response to a SEV0. After the reset, checkpoint pointer `-1` (resolves to latest valid checkpoint) failed with `ModelStoreDBRecordNotFound: Checkpoint 2137792444 : -1 does not exist.` Root: MRB reset cleared the checkpoint lineage record that `-1` depends on in ModelStore. Configerator had explicit `--checkpoint-version -1` which compounded: even after removing the config entry, the underlying -1 record was missing. Workaround: specify `--checkpoint-version <valid_version>` to bypass the missing -1 lookup; alternately stop+start (re-registration recreates the record). Denny's note: "not sure why it stopped working after yesterday's SEV0" — not yet root-caused to a specific MRB step.
