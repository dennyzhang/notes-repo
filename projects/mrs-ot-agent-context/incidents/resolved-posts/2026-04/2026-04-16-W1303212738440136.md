## Undeclared Scribe Categories - how to fix:

tldr: Online Training Jobs running with undeclared scribe categories will be terminated. This is to ensure jobs are place as efficiently as possible to avoid x-region traffic - see below with details for how to fix and get your job running again.

## Am I affected?

You may have received a few tasks with title:
*[MAST] Undeclared Scribe Category in job: mvai-training-online-<model_id>*

For example: T263807893, T263639887 and T263570440. 

Or if your online training job was stopped with the message: `Undeclared resource usage detected by MAST offline enforcement`

This is because the job needs to be relaunched with the correct scribe categories in order to ensure they are placed correctly to avoid x-region traffic.  

For more details on this enforcement see: S641880

## steps to fix: 

1. Query your model id to see what scribe category you are using: [https://fburl.com/scuba/mast_resource_enforcer/9csa8roa](https://fburl.com/scuba/mast_resource_enforcer/9csa8roa)

2. Find your training launch service config for your model:
i.e. source/aiplatform/training_launch_service/models/2128304972.cconf

3. Find --scribe-categories and replace it with the scribe categories that are actually being used. You can separate each category in the string with a |. For example: `recopublisher_features_dump|ig_clips_evergreen_media_recopublisher_feature|ig_clips_star_search_media_recopublisher_feature_v2`

4. Build and land your config diff

5. Restart Online Training through the Mast Job UI

If at step 2 you don't have a launch config, then your job is not yet productionized.  Not to worry - Simply relaunch your job with the fire cmd with the correct scribe categories.  You can find your rerun cmd under the Mast Job UI -> job defintion -> rerun_cmd_line

Please reach out to this user group and comment in post if you have any questions.
