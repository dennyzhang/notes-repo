## Root cause
_preload_item_pool() in ig_retrieval/trainer.py blocks the DPP consumer thread during startup. DPP workers fill their output queues but the trainer-side consumer does not drain them while preloading item pool → circular wait → GetDataTimeoutError after 6000s timeout → crash loop. Not a transient infra issue (reproducible across 2 regions and 9 versions over 21h). Related: D104469704 (P43 backout for similar cross-process-group scope bug). **Propose P55.**

## Mitigation
