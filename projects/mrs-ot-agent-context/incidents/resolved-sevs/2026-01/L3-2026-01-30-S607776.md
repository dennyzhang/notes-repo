## Overview
On January 5, 2026, the IG Stories Model 875799562 experienced a significant degradation in both recommendation metrics and training NE, impacting the quality of story recommendations for Instagram users. The issue was first detected via dashboard monitoring at 15:30 PST, approximately six hours after the incident began. The problem was traced to a training NEX during the publishing of snapshot 349, which led to a sharp increase in NE and a noticeable drop in model performance. This affected the product model in production, with potential but limited impact on user engagement metrics during the affected window.
Timeline and Impact
The incident unfolded over several weeks, with the initial spike in NE prompting an immediate revert to a known-good snapshot (347) and a temporary freeze on new model deployments. Recurring training was paused to prevent further propagation of the issue, and manual monitoring was instituted to ensure rapid detection of any further anomalies. The model was fully caught up with retraining by January 8, and subsequent snapshots showed stable NE and recommendation metrics. However, a second NEX event occurred on January 10 with snapshot 387, again requiring a revert and further investigation. Throughout, the team closely monitored online NE, AUC, and recommendation metrics, confirming that while there was a measurable regression, the overall user impact was contained and quickly mitigated.
Root Cause and Lessons Learned
Extensive investigation using tools like RankPilot, TDO (Training Data Observability), and IL/MPS analysis pointed to a bias explosion in the embedding_feature_modules.linear_modules.2 layer. While initial hypotheses focused on outlier dense feature values, further analysis showed that clamping and normalization did not prevent the NEX. The root cause appears to be a combination of rare data conditions and model instability, possibly triggered by subtle shifts in feature distributions or optimizer behavior. Notably, enabling gradient clipping (at 1.0 or 2.0) was found to prevent recurrence of the NEX, and this will be proposed as a permanent safeguard in future model configurations. The incident highlighted the need for robust automated alerting (MSV configuration was set to alert_only and did not block bad snapshots), improved monitoring, and the value of TDO/IL data for root cause analysis. All findings and action items are documented in the investigation doc (https://docs.google.com/document/d/1NF5KkkXBlVixpIxl0yNq035DCJryP94n03Cm37zE_NI/edit?usp=sharing). The SEV was marked as mitigated on January 30, 2026, after over two weeks of stable training and no further incidents. Going forward, we will implement gradient clipping, strengthen alerting and snapshot gating, and continue to leverage TDO for early anomaly detection to prevent similar issues.

## Root cause
The SEV was triggered by a model instability event (NEX) in the IG Stories Model 875799562 , resulting in a sharp increase in training NE and a degradation of recommendation metrics. The root cause was ultimately traced to a bias explosion in the embedding_feature_modules.linear_modules.2 layer of the model during training, as identified through extensive analysis using RankPilot, TDO (Training Data Observability), and IL/MPS data.
Technical Analysis and Investigation
Model Parameter Instability: The investigation revealed that the bias parameter in the embedding_feature_modules.linear_modules.2 layer experienced sudden, abnormal surges during the affected training runs. This was corroborated by both IL and MPS data, which showed a clear spike in the norm of this parameter coinciding with the NEX events.
Feature and Data Quality Checks: Multiple hypotheses were tested, including the presence of outlier dense feature values and embedding staleness. However, clamping and normalization of dense features did not prevent the NEX, and no consistent abnormal patterns were found in upstream embedding features or sparse features.
Optimizer and Safeguard Gaps: The model lacked sufficient gradient clipping, which allowed rare data conditions or subtle shifts in feature distributions to destabilize the training process. Enabling gradient clipping at 1.0 or 2.0 was shown to prevent recurrence of the NEX in controlled experiments.
Fundamental Cause: The fundamental issue was a combination of rare, hard-to-reproduce data conditions and insufficient model-level safeguards, leading to a bad snapshot where the model converged to an unstable state. This was not due to a specific code change or config diff, but rather a gap in robustness to outlier training dynamics.
Further details and supporting data are documented in the investigation doc (https://docs.google.com/document/d/1NF5KkkXBlVixpIxl0yNq035DCJryP94n03Cm37zE_NI/edit?usp=sharing).

## Remediation
To address the SEV impacting IG Stories Model 875799562, we took a series of immediate and structured remediation actions to restore model stability and prevent further user impact.
Immediate Mitigation Steps
• Model Revert and Training Freeze: Upon detection of the NE spike and recommendation metric degradation on January 5, 2026, we immediately reverted the production model to a known-good snapshot (347) and froze new model deployments. This action halted the propagation of the unstable model and limited further user impact.
• Recurring Training Paused: We disabled recurring training and killed ongoing flows to ensure no additional bad snapshots would be published while the root cause was under investigation.
• Manual Monitoring Instituted: The team implemented manual metric checks every 3–4 hours and daily status updates to ensure rapid detection of any further anomalies during the freeze and recovery period.
Restoration and Follow-up Actions
• Controlled Model Retraining: After reverting, we resumed model training with recent data, closely monitoring online NE, recurring NE, and recommendation metrics. The model was fully caught up by January 8, 2026, and subsequent snapshots showed stable performance.
• Snapshot Gating and Alerting Improvements: We aligned on disabling the 'alert_only' setting in MSV so that future NE threshold breaches will actively block bad snapshots from deployment, rather than only generating alerts. This is documented in the SEV chat and follow-up actions.
• Gradient Clipping Safeguard: Through controlled experiments, we found that enabling gradient clipping (at 1.0 or 2.0) prevented recurrence of the NEX. We have proposed adding this as a permanent safeguard in future model configurations, as noted in the investigation doc (https://docs.google.com/document/d/1NF5KkkXBlVixpIxl0yNq035DCJryP94n03Cm37zE_NI/edit?usp=sharing).

## Prevention
To prevent recurrence of the model instability and NE spike experienced in IG Stories Model 875799562, we have implemented and planned several concrete prevention measures, as documented in the SEV follow-up actions and investigation doc (investigation doc (https://docs.google.com/document/d/1NF5KkkXBlVixpIxl0yNq035DCJryP94n03Cm37zE_NI/edit?usp=sharing)).
Strengthening Automated Gating and Alerting
• MSV Snapshot Gating: The Model Snapshot Validator (MSV) was previously set to 'alert_only', which allowed bad snapshots to be published even when NE thresholds were exceeded. We have now aligned on disabling 'alert_only' so that MSV will actively block deployment of snapshots breaching NE thresholds, ensuring that future model instability is automatically contained before reaching production. This change is tracked in the SEV chat and follow-up actions.
• Alert Routing Audit: An audit of alerting configurations is underway to guarantee that all NE-related alerts are correctly routed to oncall rotations and cannot be silently missed. This will ensure timely detection and escalation of future anomalies.
Model-Level Safeguards
• Gradient Clipping Implementation: Controlled experiments demonstrated that enabling gradient clipping (at 1.0 or 2.0) reliably prevents the recurrence of the NEX event. We have proposed adding gradient clipping as a permanent safeguard in future model configurations, and this is being tracked for inclusion in the next MB1 update.
