## Overview
Started from 04/13, main and xapp u2m models that are at or below tier 3 got train example capped based on data age if they restarted during the period. Investigation showed this is  caused by dpp side rolling out D100836039. While the goal was to apply training data capping for models with QPS throttling, but by mistake applied to all models at tier 3 and 4. Once the change got rolled back, restarting the online job mitigate the issue.

## Incident impact
Affecting Main and X-App U2M QE models. Training data lost for due to capping for around 2 days and cause unreliable QE readings.

## Root cause
Train examples older than 5 minutes are thrown away for all QE models (tier 3 and 4) due to the bad diff from DPP D100836039.

## Remediation
The diff was rolled back due to a similar finding https://fb.workplace.com/groups/mldp.users/permalink/26458841497143504/. We restarted the affected jobs to pick up the fix.

## Prevention
Better monitoring on the train example age.
