## Root cause
[postmortem field] Certain ranks of the online training jobs crashed due to CUDACachingAllocator free_block assertion, but main processes stayed alive in zombie form, preventing the job from being shut down. Root cause: exit_w_cleanup was only called on the success path in light.py. On ChildFailedError, Python's threading._shutdown() blocked indefinitely on stuck C++ threads (NCCL collectives waiting for the dead rank). Without the agent exiting, MAST never detected the job as failed and never retried.

## Mitigation
[postmortem field] Manual restart for immediate remediation. MRS online training oncall (Denny Zhang) confirmed fix D98638473 (https://www.internalfb.com/diff/D98638473) from S628346 applies to this SEV — adds try/except on error path in light.py → exit_w_cleanup → os._exit(1), forcing process to exit regardless of NCCL thread state.
