## Root cause

[agent-feed: Opsmate Retrospection + Investigator — postmortem fields empty]

NCCL ALLGATHER collective timeout (654s) on mvai-training-online-2128873883 V9 attempt 0 (T20 Grand Teton, NHA cluster). TMS did not auto-restart (`tms_restarted=False`) because `max_cont_stucks_before_kill` was set too high (default >1), allowing the stuck job to persist without TMS killing and restarting it.

This is the 2nd occurrence — 1st was S648685 on 2026-04-13 with the same model and same NCCL failure pattern. Possible contributing factor: Kineto profiler deadlock (S640723 mitigated same day 2026-04-20), though profiler involvement was not confirmed.

## Mitigation

1. Manual restart of the training job by haoyuwu at 22:04 UTC 2026-04-20
2. D101707010 landed 17:02 UTC same day: changed `max_cont_stucks_before_kill` to 1 for online training, enabling TMS to kill and restart stuck jobs more aggressively
