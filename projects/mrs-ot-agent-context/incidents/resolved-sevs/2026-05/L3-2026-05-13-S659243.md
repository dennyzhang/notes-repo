## Root cause
[postmortem field] Upstream SEV1 S659167: QE launch of ig_enable_query_optimizer_v2 caused combinatorial explosion on the Shots tier, resulting in a 2h22m outage (05:14–07:36 AM PT). During this window, TIXU Online Training consumed corrupted/incomplete serving data, producing out-of-range calibration in both IG and FB LSR and ESR models. Models did not self-recover — corrupted data persisted in training partitions, requiring manual intervention.

5-Whys: calibration instability ← corrupted serving data ingested by OT ← Shots tier outage ← excessive query permutations ← QE ig_enable_query_optimizer_v2 launched without sufficient safeguards ← combinatorial impact underestimated in launch validation.

## Mitigation
[postmortem field]
1. Banned IG/FB TIXU training data collected 05:00–07:30 AM PT on 2026-05-05 via D103863742
2. Identified 61 impacted models; executed revert-and-ban for 20 TMS-supported models using `tms-cli create-revert-ban-plan` (reverted to snapshots prior to 04:30 AM PT)
3. Used trmgr to revert/ban ESR model snapshots
4. Audited LSR OT jobs; manually restarted 4 dead jobs: 2125877541, 2125823553, 2128227052, 2125728730
5. Continued calibration monitoring until all prod/QE/holdout models confirmed healthy
