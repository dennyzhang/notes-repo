## Incident impact
On February 9, 2026, at ~08:08 PM PST, an incorrect quota adjustment inadvertently removed 21 MACHINE_TYPE_T20_GRAND_TETON_ONLINE hosts from the critical Facebook tenant root/Facebook/Feed/ifr/ifr_tc_prod, resulting in 150% (https://pxl.cl/8V8nf) demand-to-quota oversubscription for online training hosts.
This caused 5 ONLINE CRITICAL TRAINING (https://fburl.com/scuba/mast_job_data_snapshot/f45wao62) jobs to either stall or remain in a pending state for ~12.5 hours (Feb 9, 08:08 PM PST – Feb 10, 8:46 AM PST), leading to:
Indefinite staleness in I2I training and OS Video publish pipelines for Facebook Feed
VPV drops in major generators downstream of the affected training jobs
Delayed snapshot publishes impacting model freshness for production serving

## Root cause
The root cause was a missed step during the Online/Offline quota split process, compounded by an incorrect correction during a separate SEV mitigation.

The Quota Split Process
On Feb 9 starting at ~11:50 AM PST, the MAST team was performing an Online/Offline quota split for Facebook tenant reservations. The goal was to carve out dedicated "online" quota for each tenant so that online training jobs would not be preempted due to reservation unavailability. The process for each tenant was:
Add a new online quota reservation (e.g., MACHINE_TYPE_T20_GRAND_TETON_ONLINE).
Enable a JK (feature flag) for the tenant to start using the online quota.
Deduct the same amount from the original reservation (MACHINE_TYPE_T20_GRAND_TETON).

The Error
When performing these steps for the tenant root/Facebook/Feed/ifr/ifr_tc_prod(GTT quota:56), the team allocated 21 MACHINE_TYPE_T20_GRAND_TETON_ONLINE but missed step 3 — the corresponding deduction of 21 from MACHINE_TYPE_T20_GRAND_TETON was never made. This left the tenant oversubscribed by 21 hosts on paper (77 total: 21 online + 56 regular, against a fleet supply of 56). No immediate impact was observed because there were no non-online jobs running on the tenant at the time.
The team then correctly completed all 3 steps for the child tenant root/Facebook/Feed/ifr/ifr_tc_prod/ifr_tc_prod_high_qps, allocating 40 online and deducting 40 from regular. The resulting quotas at the parent tenant level rolled up to 61 MACHINE_TYPE_T20_GRAND_TETON_ONLINE (21 + 40) and 16 MACHINE_TYPE_T20_GRAND_TETON (56 - 40).

The Compounding Incident
Later that day at ~2:25 PM, a separate SEV S620238 occurred due to an incorrect GrandTeton capacity transfer from Recsys to GenAI. That SEV was mitigated at ~7:48 PM by reverting the transfer, but an oversubscription of ~36 GrandTeton hosts persisted. While investigating, the team identified the surplus at ifr_tc_prod — it appeared to have 77 hosts (61 online + 16 regular) against a supply of 56. Assuming this was the source of the remaining oversubscription, the team reduced MACHINE_TYPE_T20_GRAND_TETON_ONLINE by 21 at the parent tenant at ~8:08 PM.

The Impact
However, all 61 online quota were actively in use — 21 by jobs running directly on ifr_tc_prod and 40 by jobs on the child tenant ifr_tc_prod_high_qps. Removing 21 online quota from the parent tenant left the 5 online training jobs running at that level without sufficient quota, causing them to enter a pending state. This went undetected until Feb 10 at ~7 AM, when the Facebook team reported model staleness. The issue was mitigated at ~8:46 AM by restoring the 21 MACHINE_TYPE_T20_GRAND_TETON_ONLINE quota to the tenant.

How Root Cause Was Identified
The root cause was identified through the following steps:
Facebook team reported VPV drops and model staleness on the morning of Feb 10, initially suspected to be related to the earlier SEV S620238.
IFR Oncall investigation revealed delayed snapshot publishes and 5 training jobs stuck in pending state at ifr_tc_prod.
Tracing back the quota changes, the team identified that the 21 online quota reduction made at ~8:08 PM on Feb 9 (to mitigate SEV S620238) had removed capacity that was actively serving production jobs.
	
Root Cause#2 : On Feb 8th, 4:42 PM PST, there was around 16 Grand Teton were transferred from root/Facebook/Feed/ifr/ifr_tc_prod/ifr_tc_prod_high_qps to root/Facebook/Feed/ifr/ifr_tc/ifr_tc_hstu https://fburl.com/scuba/mast_aicm_registry_capacity_events/wxuyafl5 . This also reduced the capacity from 72 to 56. If we had this capacity not transferred, this could have been used for the online training jobs.

## Remediation
To immediately address the outage affecting Tier 1 model training and publish jobs in the IFR_TC_PROD tenant, mast capacity oncall manually reverted (https://www.internalfb.com/phabricator/paste/view/P2178736923) the recent quota reduction that had removed 21 MACHINE_TYPE_T20_GRAND_TETON_ONLINE capacity from the tenant. This action restored the quota to its previous state, allowing all impacted jobs to resume running and mitigating the staleness in I2I training and OS Video publish. 

After this followup continued to fix the incorrect quota and be able to correctly adjust the online and offline quotas to the correct values to fix the over subscription issue this immediate mitigation triggered again.
