## Overview
The cogwheel_trunk_multi_arm_metrics_online_test step on mvai/video_udd_lsr conveyor failed from Feb 27 through Mar 11. Online training publish subprocesses were crashing (.so load failure / dependency conflict) and the parent process had no liveness detection, blocking forever on message_queue.get() until Gloo timeout triggered StuckJobException. Troubleshooting was impacted by other unrelated errors causing exceptions before this on subsequent runs, and the error condition changing for the same root cause due to another diff landing and removing the StuckJobException entirely and exposing the Gloo timeout.

## Incident impact
Trunk releases for mvai/video_udd_lsr blocked for ~6.5 days (~159 hours), delaying MLE/SWE iteration on video UDD models. 24 consecutive cogwheel online test failures (R4473 through R4571). No user-facing quality degradation.

## Root cause
The subprocess crashed at dlopen time — two different CUDA libraries (fbr/flash and ads_mkl) both try to register the same fbgemm::fmha_bwd operator. This is a build/packaging
conflict in the app-layer or base-layer: the Blackwell FMHA backward operator is defined in two separate .cu files and both got linked into the publish subprocess's binary.

## Remediation
Runs began passing and the issue disappeared without any action from us (https://fburl.com/conveyor/qk7ipzje)

Remediation took several days because of other issues on this trunk causing errors before StuckJobException and other SEVs on supported conveyors for MRS Release Oncall.
