## Incident impact
Across IFR T2I, ESR and IGR T2I we are seeing the case of Silvertorch Publish jobs successfully running the steps in publish (kmeans, index bulk eval, etc) and uploading a snapshot to manifold. But the MAST job continues to idle / hang for several hours (e.g. 9 hours for T2I).

## Root cause
Some change between light_cli:4539 -> v4535 triggered behavior where a py-spy process would enter a condition that would cause it to consume the core it is running on and not respond to TERM signals anymore (https://pxl.cl/90S6j)

py-spy is run via the subprocess module from the trainer. During shutdown the trainer sends a TERM signal to py-spy and expects it to gracefully shutdown. Due to the hang py-spy does not shutdown gracefully, and as a result the trainer does not exit.

We are explicitly choosing to not root-cause the py-spy hang as it is a third-party open source tool and it will imminently deprecated in AI Training. See the Prevention section.

## Remediation
Once we identified py-spy as a potential cause we used the the global kill switch jk (https://www.internalfb.com/intern/justknobs/?name=ai_infra%2Fpy_spy#enable) to disable py-spy for Andrew Mao -- this confirmed that with py-spy disabled the jobs now shutdown as expected.

We then disabled py-spy for all of MVAI (see Prevention, below)

## Prevention
Once we confirmed that py-spy was indeed the reason that the trainer was not shutting down we changed the shutdown handling logic to wait 15 seconds at the TERM signal and then send a KILL signal: D94098324 (https://www.internalfb.com/diff/D94098324)

Long-term: py-spy was implemented mid-2025 in response to AI Infra wanting better visibility into what the trainer processes were doing. py-spy is a third-party open-source tool and the mechanism for launching it was never the ideal long term pattern. It was intended to be a stop-gap while we worked with the strobelight team to build a better solution that was out-of-band of the trainer process, like all other strobelight profiling.

Earlier this year we announced that we were launching this new solution built on top of strobelight: https://fb.workplace.com/groups/2039331519542045/permalink/3783185691823277/ 

We have also decided that we will sunset py-spy in early Q2 in favor of mast-job-walltime. Due to that decision we have opted to simply leave py-spy disabled for all of MVAI as the long term prevention.
